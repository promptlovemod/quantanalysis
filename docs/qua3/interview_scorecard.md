# Founding Engineer Interview Scorecard

Scale: 1-4
- 1: below bar
- 2: mixed signal
- 3: meets bar
- 4: exceeds bar

## Categories
- 0-to-1 ownership
- Ingestion/system design reliability
- Coding/testing quality
- Product/domain reasoning
- Collaboration/communication

Hiring guideline:
- No category <2
- At least three categories >=3
- System design and coding/testing must be >=3
